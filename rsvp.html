FAQ

Helpful answers

What should I wear?

Dress in a comfortable tropical or elegant evening style. Light fabrics and warm-weather attire are ideal.

Do I need a visa?

Depending on your nationality, you may need a visa or e-visa to enter Indonesia. Please check the latest requirements before travel.

Will there be transportation from the airport?

We’ll provide travel guidance and encourage pre-arranged airport transfers for a smoother arrival.

Is Bali weather hot?

Expect warm tropical weather, especially during the day. Bring light clothing, sunscreen, and a coverup for evenings.
