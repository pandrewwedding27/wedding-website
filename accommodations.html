Venue & Stay

Our Venue

The Surga Villa Estate · Uluwatu

Perched on the clifftops above Melasti Beach, The Surga is a private estate with two luxury villas, 13 suites in total, a 25-metre infinity pool, expansive lawns, and sweeping views of the Indian Ocean. This is where we'll be celebrating, and where many of you will be staying.

Photos courtesy of The Surga Villa Estate via Bali.Love.

Also Staying Nearby

Bebas Villas · Bingin, Uluwatu

A collection of modern tropical villas and suites tucked among cottonwood, fruit, and palm trees, with a 14-metre pool and plenty of room for groups and families. A peaceful, leafy alternative to the clifftop.

Photos courtesy of Bebas Villas.

Accommodation

We will be providing accommodation for guests staying at either The Surga Villa Estate or Bebas Villas. We'll be assigning rooms and will share details closer to the date.

Prefer to find your own place? You're more than welcome to! Uluwatu has plenty of options, from boutique hotels to cliffside stays. Just let us know in your RSVP so we can plan transport and keep everyone together for the celebrations.
