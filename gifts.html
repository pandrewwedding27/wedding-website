Travel

Getting to Bali

Flights

Most guests will arrive through Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Bali. We recommend booking flights early to secure the best rates and travel times.

Airport transfers

Private transfers are the easiest option for arriving in Bali. We’ll share group transfer details closer to the event date.

Helpful details

Before you go

  • Check passport validity and visa requirements well in advance.
  • Bring your travel documents and accommodations confirmation.
  • Plan for warm tropical weather and light, breathable clothing.
  • Consider carrying cash for local transport, markets, and small purchases.
