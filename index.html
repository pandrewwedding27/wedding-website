Getting to Bali

Practical information for your journey

✈
Flights

Most guests will arrive through Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar. We recommend booking flights early to secure the best rates and travel times. Direct flights are available from major cities worldwide.

🚗
Airport Transfers

Private transfers are the most convenient option for arrival. We'll share group transfer details closer to the event date to ensure a smooth and hassle-free arrival experience for all our guests.

📋
Visa & Documents

Check your passport validity well in advance. Most nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival or apply for an e-visa online. We recommend checking with your local Indonesian embassy for specific requirements.

💡
Travel Tips

Pack light, tropical clothing, bring high SPF sunscreen, and consider travel insurance. The weather will be warm and tropical, so plan accordingly for comfort and protection from the sun.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Elegant tropical and resort attire is perfect. Think light fabrics, flowing dresses, linen suits, and breathable materials. We'll send more specific dress code details with your RSVP confirmation.

Do I need travel insurance?

We recommend travel insurance to cover any unexpected circumstances. It's a small investment that can provide peace of mind for your international travel.

Is the wedding kid-friendly?

We'd love to celebrate with your children! Please indicate their ages when you RSVP so we can plan accordingly and ensure everyone has a wonderful time.

What's the weather like in August?

August is Bali's dry season with warm, sunny days (around 85-90°F). It's perfect weather for celebrations and outdoor activities. Bring sunscreen and light, breathable clothing.

Can I bring a plus one?

We're keeping our celebration intimate with our nearest and dearest. Your invitation will specify if you're able to bring a guest. Please reach out if you have questions!

Do I need a visa?

Most nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival or through an e-visa application online. Check the Indonesian embassy website for your specific country's requirements well in advance.
