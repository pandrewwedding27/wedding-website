Transportation

  Arriving at the Airport

Fly into Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar. Both The Surga and Bebas Villas are in Uluwatu, on Bali's Bukit Peninsula, so expect a drive of an hour or more depending on traffic. Check-in at your accommodation opens at 3:00 PM on Tuesday, August 3.

  Airport Transfers

We're planning group transfers from the airport to The Surga and Bebas Villas. Details to be announced – we'll ask for your flight information closer to the date. If you'd rather arrange your own ride, private drivers and taxis are available at the airport.

  Getting Around During the Celebrations

We'll arrange transport for the wedding events, including between The Surga and Bebas Villas. Details to be announced. If you'd like to explore on your own, ride-hailing apps like Grab and Gojek and private drivers by the day are easy ways to get around.

  Departing

Check-out is by 11:00 AM on Friday, August 6. We'll share departure transfer details closer to the wedding. See the Travel page for ideas on what to do next.
